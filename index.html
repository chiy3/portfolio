About Eric.

I'm a Staff UX Designer at Walmart. I led the design of Walmart's first fully AI-powered customer care platform, and I work on the systems behind it: the care application agents use every day, the workflow engine that builds their processes, and the content system that writes their answers.

I measure the work in business outcomes: shorter handling times, lower training costs, and hours given back to the teams I design for. Before Walmart, I worked on network automation at Cisco and managed databases at Rackspace's ObjectRocket.

Much of this work is confidential. The case studies are summaries, and I'm glad to walk through any of them in more depth on a call.
