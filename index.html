About Eric.

I solve problems carefully and measure the result in business outcomes: shorter handling times, lower training costs, hours given back to the teams I design for.

At Walmart I work on the platform agents use to help customers every day: the care application itself, the workflow engine and content system behind it, and now the move to AI-assisted care. Before that I worked on network automation at Cisco and managed databases at Rackspace's ObjectRocket.

Much of this work is confidential. The case studies here are summaries, and I'm glad to walk through any of them in detail.
