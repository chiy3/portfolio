About Eric.

I design for simplicity: breaking complex problems down into clear experiences.

I've spent 6+ years in product design, across managed databases at Rackspace's ObjectRocket, network automation at Cisco and customer care at Walmart. I believe good design makes the complex feel simple, so people can focus on the work, not the tool.

Much of this work is confidential. The case studies are summaries, and I'm glad to walk through any of them in more depth on a call.

Based in the San Francisco Bay Area. Outside of work, I'm on cat duty: my cat, , is my favorite coworker and my toughest design critic.
