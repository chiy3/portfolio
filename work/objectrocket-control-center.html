Overview

ObjectRocket, a Rackspace Technology company, provides fully managed database-as-a-service. Mission Control is its main product: it takes on the complexity of running databases so businesses can focus on building and scaling their applications.

I evaluated and redesigned the experience of creating a database instance. The new design reduced task completion time by 25% and raised customer satisfaction by 30%.
