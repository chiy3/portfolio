Overview

Fast access to accurate content drives efficiency and continuous improvement. Walmart's Content Management System is designed to maximize reuse and cut the cost of creating and maintaining content. By breaking information into smaller reusable components, it lets writers create, update and maintain content quickly and consistently.

Challenge

Each year the content team handles more than 10,000 requests to update or create content across Walmart, including communications, store help centers, IVR, chatbots and more. Writers relied on nine different tools to create, maintain, publish and store it. Because every piece of content was treated individually, two problems followed.

High maintenance costs and duplicated effort

The same information appears in many places. The return policy, for example, shows up in more than 100 articles across stores, the help center, the eCommerce knowledge base, customer texts and emails. When it changes, say from a 30-day to a 90-day window, every one of those articles has to be updated.

Slow speed to market and compliance risk

Every article was written from scratch and went through multiple reviews. Even a simple two-page article could take 270 minutes (4.5 hours), and longer when legal review was needed.

Solution

The system is built on single-source authoring and centralized omni-publishing across business lines and audiences. Content is broken into modular elements that are assembled into collections and published as different deliverables, such as articles and customer-facing messages.

The same elements can be reused across collections and published to many audiences and platforms. That reduces inconsistency, removes duplication and speeds up delivery for writers, employees, clients and customers.

Impact

With 70% content reusability on a single platform, the product delivers more than $1 million a year in authoring efficiency and saves over 20,000 hours annually.
